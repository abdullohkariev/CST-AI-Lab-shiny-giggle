How it works

You write a prompt or add audio. The tool sends it to an AI model, and the result appears on the page, ready to download. Everything is free and needs no account. Images are drawn by volunteer computers on the AI Horde network, so they can take a little while. Text to Speech and Speech to Text run inside your browser, so your text and recordings never leave your device.

About

Lumo is a school project from the Applied AI lesson at Canadian School of Tashkent, created by Abdulloh Kariev.
