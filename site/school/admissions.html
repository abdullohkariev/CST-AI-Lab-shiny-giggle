Admissions 2026–2027

Five Steps from Enquiry to Enrollment

Admissions is a conversation, not a screening. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis, and mid-year entry is possible when seats remain.

1

Enquire

Send a quick form, call or email, and receive a welcome pack.

2

Visit Campus

Join an Open House or book a private tour and meet the teachers.

3

Apply

Submit the application with transcripts, ID and two photos.

4

Assessment

Grade 1 and up take an English and maths placement, plus a family interview.

5

Enrolled

Receive an offer letter, sign the agreement and welcome to CST.

📅 First day of the 2026–2027 school year

Wednesday, 2 September 2026, the day after Independence Day. High School seats are limited, so early application is encouraged.

Entry Points

A Place at Every Stage

Admissions is a conversation, not a screening. We are listening for fit, not testing against a checklist.

Lower Primary

Grades K–2 · Ages 5–8. Rolling admission, placed by age on 1 September. A friendly campus visit and informal classroom observation.

Upper Primary

Grades 3–5 · Ages 8–11. English and maths placement, a family interview and a language proficiency check where needed.

Middle School

Grades 6–9 · Ages 11–15. English, maths and science placement plus recent transcripts and a family interview.

High School

Grades 10–12 · Ages 15–18. Transcript review against Nova Scotia standards. Seats are limited, so apply early.
