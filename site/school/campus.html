Our Campus

Spaces Built for How Students Learn

Everything for the whole school sits on one campus in Tashkent’s Mirabad District.

🧪Science

Three Science Labs

Dedicated biology, chemistry and physics laboratories where science is done with your hands.

→
📚Reading

Library for Every Age

Print and digital collections, reading nooks and research support, from picture books to senior projects.

→
🤖Technology

Innovation & Robotics Lab

3D printers, electronics and build kits, and home to the school’s FLL and FTC robotics teams.

→
🎙️Media

Creative & Media Spaces

A content studio, a podcast studio and a virtual-reality lab for making and broadcasting.

→
🏊Sport

Friday at the Gymnasium

Every Friday all students train at a partner gym: football, tennis, swimming, badminton and basketball.

→
🍎Wellbeing

Dining Hall

Breakfast, lunch and snacks cooked in the school’s own kitchen, with a vegetarian main every day.

→
Dining & Cafeteria

Fresh Food, Made on Campus

Three on-campus meals a day, cooked in the school’s own kitchen and shared in one hall.

🍳

Breakfast · 8:00

Eggs, porridge or a hot grain with fruit, yogurt, bread and tea or milk, served before the first lesson.

🍲

Lunch · 12:30

A complete hot meal with protein, a grain, two vegetables and a salad station. A vegetarian main is offered daily.

🍎

Snacks

Fruit, a baked item or a savoury portion to bridge energy gaps, with a lighter version for students staying for clubs.
