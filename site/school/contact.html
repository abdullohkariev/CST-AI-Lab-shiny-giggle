Before You Write

Frequently Asked, Quickly Answered

How do I arrange a campus tour?

Register for the next Open House or ask for a private tour. We will confirm a time with you directly.

Can my child join mid-year?

Yes, subject to places in the year group. Admissions will review your child’s records and advise on timing.

Where is the school?

4 Ferghana Yuli, Mirabad District, Tashkent. One campus serves the whole school.

Which languages does the team speak?

English, Russian and Uzbek. Write or call in whichever is most comfortable.

What documents do I need?

Typically identification documents and recent school records. Admissions will guide you step by step.

How quickly will you reply?

Within one working day, Monday to Friday. Weekend messages are answered on Monday.
