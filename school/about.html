About CST

A Canadian school taking root in Tashkent

Canadian School of Tashkent (CST) is Uzbekistan’s first Canadian school. It is a single K–12 school that combines Canadian academic rigour, an international outlook and Uzbek heritage, all at once and none at the expense of the others.

  • Class sizes capped at twenty, so every child is known by name
  • Teachers who eat lunch with students and attend their concerts
  • Thirty-four nationalities learning side by side
  • Licensed in Uzbekistan and in Nova Scotia, Canada
🎯

Recognised on Three Fronts

Licenses & Memberships

🇺🇿

Uzbekistan

Licensed by the Ministry of Preschool and School Education for Kindergarten through Grade 12.

🍁

Nova Scotia, Canada

Grades 10–12 are licensed by the Province of Nova Scotia. Graduates earn the Nova Scotia High School Diploma.

🌐

Council of International Schools

CST is a CIS member, committed to international standards in teaching, safeguarding and wellbeing.
