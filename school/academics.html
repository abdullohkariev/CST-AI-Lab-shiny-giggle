Academics

One Continuous Journey, Ages 5 to 18

A rigour-first curriculum, traditional where tradition serves the child and progressive where progress does.

K–2

Lower Primary · Ages 5–8

Everything starts with curiosity. A daily literacy block, Singapore-style maths, and an integrated Inquiry subject, with a homeroom teacher and co-teacher in every class.

3–5

Upper Primary · Ages 8–11

Curiosity sharpens into inquiry. Science and Social Studies become their own subjects, and Uzbek joins Russian so every child grows up trilingual.

6–9

Middle School · Ages 11–15

Inquiry matures into independence. Specialist teachers, lab science, a Science Fair research project and the start of the Future-Ready courses.

10–12

High School · Ages 15–18

Independence becomes a passport. The Nova Scotia program leads to a diploma recognised by universities around the world.

🍁 The Nova Scotia Pathway

In Grades 10–12 students follow the same provincial curriculum as their peers in Halifax, with courses such as Pre-Calculus, Chemistry, Physics, Biology, Canadian History and Global Politics. Graduates may leave with three qualifications: the Nova Scotia High School Diploma, the Uzbekistan National Certificate and the CST Diploma.
