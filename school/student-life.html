Student Life

More Than a School, a Community

Every student commits to at least one co-curricular activity each year, because character is built after the bell rings.

⚽

Athletics & Sports

Football, basketball and volleyball teams, plus swimming, athletics, gymnastics, martial arts and mountain trips.

🎭

Arts & Performance

Visual arts studios, up to two stage productions a year, choir and instrumental ensembles.

🤖

Robotics

FIRST LEGO League for younger students and FIRST Tech Challenge for seniors.

🌐

Model UN

Students debate in English and Russian, with delegations at international conferences.

💚

Volunteers Program

Monthly community service for Middle and High School, including tutoring and local partnerships.

🕒

After School

Supervised study, clubs and training, with students under staff care until pickup.
